Academic Clarification: R-Squared ($R^2$) vs. Classification Accuracy

Why $R^2$ is NOT "accuracy": In supervised Machine Learning, classification models (predicting categories) evaluate accuracy as percentage of correct classes. Continuous regression models (predicting continuous dollar/rupee amounts) evaluate the coefficient of determination $R^2$, calculated as:

$$R^2 = 1 - \frac{SS_{\text{res}}}{SS_{\text{tot}}} = 1 - \frac{\sum (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2}{\sum (y_i - \bar{y})^2}$$

An $R^2$ score of {{ "%.4f"|format(metrics.metrics.r2_score) }} indicates that approximately {{ "%.1f"|format(metrics.metrics.r2_score * 100) }}% of the variance in residential house prices is accounted for by our linear combination of property features. Performance depends on dataset quality, homoscedasticity, and real-world market generalization.
